ABOUT OUR PROJECT

A Sustainable Approach to Clean Water

SolarAqua explores how solar energy can be used to support water purification. By using energy from the sun, these systems can help reduce dependence on conventional electricity.

The purification method depends on the water source and the contaminants that need to be removed.

Explore the Process

THE PURIFICATION PROCESS

How Does Solar Water Purification Work?

A solar-powered purification system uses energy from sunlight to operate or support a water treatment process.

01. Solar Energy Collection

Solar panels capture sunlight and convert it into electrical energy.

02. Water Treatment

The system uses an appropriate treatment method, such as filtration or solar-powered distillation, depending on its design.

03. Treated Water Output

Water passes through the selected treatment process. Its quality should be tested to confirm that it meets the intended use.

WHY SOLAR POWER

Benefits of Solar-Powered Purification

Solar energy can support water treatment while helping reduce dependence on conventional electricity.

Renewable Energy

Sunlight is a renewable energy source that can help power suitable purification systems.

Reduced Grid Dependence

Solar-powered systems may operate in locations where access to electricity is limited.

Environmentally Conscious

Using solar energy can reduce reliance on electricity generated from fossil fuels.

WHERE CAN IT BE USED?

Applications of Solar Water Purification

Solar-powered purification may be useful in different settings, depending on the system's capacity and treatment technology.

Rural Communities

Supporting water treatment in areas with limited electricity infrastructure.

Remote Locations

Providing an alternative energy source for suitable water treatment equipment.

Educational Projects

Demonstrating renewable energy and water treatment concepts in schools and colleges.

INSIDE THE SYSTEM

Main Components

Solar Panel

Converts sunlight into electrical energy to power compatible equipment.

Water Pump

Moves water through the system when a pump is included in the design.

Purification Unit

Uses the selected treatment technology, such as filtration or distillation.

Water Storage Tank

Stores treated water when storage is included in the system.
